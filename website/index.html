Gevurah Boxing

A rhythm boxing game for Android and iOS. Read the tell, answer on the beat, and knock down the struggles that stand in your way.

Coming soon Ages 13 and over

How it plays

Read, answer, strike

Every opponent shows a tell before it moves. Guard, slip or take it on the beat, then strike when the opening comes.

Struggles, not people

Fear, Worry, Anger, Doubt, Pride and more appear as abstract shadow forms. Each one fights in its own way, and each one can be knocked down.

Verse cards are your strategy

Before a bout you equip three verse cards. Each one changes how a move behaves, and each has a trade-off.

Calm, stylised fighting

No blood. Impact flashes and stars, and a defeated struggle lifts like a weight released. Verses appear only in the pauses, never over live action.

Free to play

The game is free. Ads appear on menu screens and at natural breaks, never during a fight. A one-time purchase removes them. No purchase gives a gameplay advantage.
