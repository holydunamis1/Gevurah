Privacy policy

Effective 18 November 2026. This policy covers the Gevurah Boxing app (app ID com.zdmgold.gevurah) and this website.

The short version: no accounts, no analytics, progress stays on your phone, and the only data that leaves it is what the ad system needs to show ads, and only where you allow it.

What the game stores on your phone

Your progress, stars, earned verse cards, settings, daily-bout history and purchase status are stored on your phone. We do not receive them. Uninstalling the game removes them.

Accounts

There are no accounts, sign-in, profiles or chat. We do not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Ads

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may use your device’s advertising ID, IP address and device and app information. Ads never appear during a fight. Buying Remove Ads stops banner and interstitial ads.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game shows Google’s consent message before any ad is requested, and you can review or change your choice later under Privacy choices in Settings. On iOS, the game may ask permission to track you across other companies’ apps and websites; if you say no, the game works the same.

Google’s own information about how it uses data from apps that use its services is at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Your phone’s settings let you reset or limit your advertising ID.

Purchases

The Remove Ads purchase is processed by Google Play or the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. The game reads your purchase status from the store so it can remove ads and restore the purchase.

Notifications

The daily-bout reminder is optional. It is a notification the game schedules on your phone; nothing is sent from a server. The game asks for permission only when you turn the reminder on.

Scripture text

Verses are bundled inside the game and work offline. The game does not send which verses you read anywhere.

Small settings file

The game may fetch a small public settings file from this website, for example to switch off a broken feature. Like any web request, this lets the website host see your IP address. The request does not include any identifier from the game.

Crash information

The game does not use a crash-reporting service. It keeps a short log on your phone. If you choose to send it to us by email from Settings, we receive only what the email contains.

Age

Gevurah Boxing is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13.

Sharing

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with anyone except the ad and store services described above.

Your choices

  • Say no to the consent message or to tracking where it is offered.
  • Change your choice under Privacy choices in Settings, where shown.
  • Reset or limit your advertising ID in your phone settings.
  • Uninstall the game to delete everything stored on your phone.

Changes

If the game’s behaviour changes in a way that affects this policy, this page is updated before the new version is released, and the effective date above changes.

Contact

Questions about this policy: see the support page.
