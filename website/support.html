Common questions

How do I remove ads?

Open Settings in the game and choose Remove Ads. It is a one-time purchase made through Google Play or the App Store.

I bought Remove Ads and it is gone after reinstalling or changing phones.

Open Settings and choose Restore purchase, signed in to the same store account you bought with.

How do I see my privacy choices?

If you are in a region where a consent choice is required, Settings shows a Privacy choices row where you can review or change your answer.

How do I turn off the daily reminder?

Switch it off in the game Settings, or turn off notifications for the game in your phone settings.

Where is my progress stored?

On your phone only. There are no accounts, so progress does not move between devices.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstalling the game removes the progress and settings stored on your phone.
